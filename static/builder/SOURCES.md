# Showcase photography

These photographs are used only by the fictional **Halden & Moss Builders** showcase at
`/p/contractor` (`src/lib/templates/contractor/defaults.ts`). They are stock images, not the
work of any real firm, and they are never shown on a derived prospect page: `derive.ts` leaves
`photography` null and `projects` empty. Replace them with a client's real project photography
before a site goes live.

All are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use, no attribution required). Each
file was downsized and recompressed. Page links below come from Unsplash search listings; confirm
the photographer on the page if you want to credit them.
